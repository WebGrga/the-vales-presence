# House rules

The book this studio learns from. Every agent stage reads it before it starts work. Where a rule here and a skill disagree, the rule wins, unless a video shows the rule is wrong: then the agent says so and fixes the rule.

You can change any of it in the Context tab. Your version is kept in your data folder; this shipped copy stays as it is, and "Reset to default" brings it back.

## How we cut

*Your taste, in your own words. How tight you like the talk, what a pause is for, how many zooms are too many, what the captions look like. Write one rule per list item. The agent follows these on every video. Empty means the skills decide.*

## Tools we use

*What works on this computer: a flag that fixed a render, a font that is installed, a model that runs well here. The agent adds a line when it finds something, with the date; you can add your own.*

## Lessons

*What the agent learned from your corrections. It writes one only when a correction shows a way of working that would go wrong again on another video, in the same turn you corrected it. Each lesson is one list item ending with its date, the evidence, and two counts:*

*`- The rule, as a reason. _(2026-01-31; evidence: what happened, in a few words; kept 0, against 0)_`*

*`kept` goes up when you approve work that followed the lesson; `against` when a correction goes against it. A lesson judged wrong more often than it held up is rewritten or dropped. The Context tab lists them with their counts.*

- Pick the topic before the picture: a rising tool, a news spike or a search that is growing, and be early; a weak premise stays mid-pack whatever the thumbnail, and packaging adds about 2 points to a strong one. _(2026-11-21; evidence: blind panels of a same-channel feed; kept 0, against 0)_
- Judge a title as text, among real winners, before any picture is drawn, and draw thumbnails only for the titles at or above the 3rd-best real one; if a package fails, fix the premise, not the picture. _(2026-11-21; evidence: picture redesigns moved weak premises by 0 to 1 places; kept 0, against 0)_
- Ask whether a stranger knows what the video is about and why to click before you make or show any option: you know the context, the viewer does not, and a plan's concepts are a start, not a spec. _(2026-11-21; evidence: most options in one feed test were marked unclear; kept 0, against 0)_
- Title and picture tell one story: the topic in plain words plus the hook, never the same words twice, and a number on the picture needs its meaning next to it. _(2026-11-21; evidence: feed-test notes; kept 0, against 0)_
- Three elements at most on a thumbnail (a big face or the real logo, the real screen big, one thick line of text on a box), counting every chip, badge and arrow; enlarge them until no big empty ground is left. _(2026-11-21; evidence: the liked tiles were the ones with the fewest elements; kept 0, against 0)_
- Early beats good: ship the first honest package to the feed test and learn from the marks; a second round on a weak premise costs more than a new premise. _(2026-11-21; evidence: gated premises passed on round one, ungated ones needed two redesigns; kept 0, against 0)_
- A face alone is no edge: match it to the video's feeling, and use it only when it says something (a gesture, a reaction). Without a face, use the real logo or the real screen. _(2026-11-21; evidence: blind judges called stiff cutouts cheap; kept 0, against 0)_
- Never fake proof with an AI-made screen, interface or face; use a real capture. A generated background is fine, but a background behind a screen added nothing in tests: use a picture model only when the scene is the subject. _(2026-11-21; evidence: blind panels tied or lost with generated backdrops; kept 0, against 0)_
- Keep the bottom-right corner empty (the duration badge covers it) and check every thumbnail at 160 by 90 before it is shown. _(2026-11-21; evidence: a skipped small-size check lost a round; kept 0, against 0)_
- A format decays: before you copy a title shape, check how its recent videos did compared with its old ones. _(2026-11-21; evidence: a format's views fell sharply within a week; kept 0, against 0)_
