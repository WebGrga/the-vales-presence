# Script a video

The script is what the user will say, in their voice, following `studio/plan.md` beat by beat. It is a tool for recording, not prose.

## Shape of studio/script.md

```markdown
## 1. Hook (0:00)
[on screen: the finished app running]
The line they say. Short. One idea per line.

## 2. The rules (0:25)
[on screen: ...]
...
```

- One heading per beat from the plan, with a rough start time (150 spoken words ≈ 1 minute).
- `[on screen: ...]` lines say what is shown, so the edit can follow.
- Lines short enough to say in one breath; contractions; no words the user would not say out loud.
- The first two lines confirm the click: say or show the thumbnail's promise.
- Each beat ends with a turn into the next ("but", "so", a question) instead of "next, ...".
- Cut what deflates: apologies, self-deprecation, long setup, tangents.

## Rules

- If the user wrote or edited `studio/script.md` in the Studio, their words win: tighten and mark suggestions instead of rewriting. Put a suggestion on its own line starting with `> suggestion:`.
- Never invent results, numbers or quotes the user has not given or shown.
- Keep it to the plan's length; say how long it reads (the Studio shows words and minutes).
